About & credits

Rhema (ῥῆμα, "word") teaches New Testament Koine Greek with spaced retrieval and real text from day one. It is a static site: nothing you do here leaves your device.

Greek text: SBL Greek New Testament. Copyright © 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software (CC BY 4.0). Morphology, lemmas and glosses: MorphGNT — Tauber, J. K., ed. (2017) MorphGNT: SBLGNT Edition, v6.12 (CC BY-SA 3.0), with glosses from the public-domain Dodson lexicon.

Reference translations: World English Bible (public domain; "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org), King James Version (1769) and Young's Literal Translation (public domain).

Pronunciation schemes: Erasmian (the common seminary scheme) and reconstructed Koine (after Randall Buth, Biblical Language Center).
