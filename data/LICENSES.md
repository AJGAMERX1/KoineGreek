# Data sources and licenses

Everything under `data/` is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from the sources
below, plus hand-authored reference grammar in `scripts/curriculum.py`
(alphabet, paradigms, principal parts, lesson sequencing and rule text).

Morphological analysis and lemmatization: **CC BY-SA 3.0** | "Scripture quotations marked SBLGNT are from the SBL Greek New Testament. ## Rules

* **Never** add ESV, NIV, NASB, NRSV, CSB or any other copyrighted translation to `data/`.
* Keep the SBLGNT attribution line and the MorphGNT citation visible in the app's About/credits screen.
* CC BY-SA content (morphology, lexicon glosses) means derived data files must stay shareable under a compatible license.
* Regenerate rather than hand-edit generated files: `python3 scripts/build_data.py`.
